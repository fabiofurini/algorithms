# Asymptotic growth and notation

*Part 3 of the course, volume 3 of the lecture notes.* How the functions of a natural variable that count the steps of an algorithm grow: the families of increasing functions (logarithms, powers, exponentials, factorial), the hierarchy of infinities and the comparison of orders of growth; the asymptotic notations Theta, big O, Omega, little o and omega, their properties and their use in the analysis of algorithms. Limits and functions of a natural variable are taken from the course of Mathematical Analysis 1.
