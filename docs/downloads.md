# Downloads

The lecture notes and the exercise books in PDF, updated at every publication of the site, with the same numbering of definitions, theorems, examples and exercises as the website. Text and figures are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en).

## Lecture notes

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 1 · Foundations and basic algorithms**

    ---

    The representation of numbers, pseudo-code and an introduction to C++; the first algorithms: sorting (insertion, exchange and selection sort), linear and binary search, numerical algorithms (change of base, primality test, sieve of Eratosthenes, greatest common divisor). Every algorithm with its pseudo-code, examples executed step by step and the C++ code.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-1-foundations.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-1-foundations.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 2 · Analysis and recursion**

    ---

    The analysis of algorithms: input size, running time and asymptotic notation, correctness with loop invariants. Recursion: recurrence relations, the Master Theorem, recursive algorithms (array sum, merge sort, binary search, maximum subarray) and the comparison between recursive and iterative versions.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-2-analysis-recursion.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-2-analysis-recursion.pdf)

</div>
