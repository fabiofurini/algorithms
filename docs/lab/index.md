# Interactive lab

Here you can watch the algorithms at work. Each graph has **sliders** and buttons:
◀ and ▶ execute one step at a time, «Run» goes on by itself until the end, and next to the
drawing you find the values of the variables and the line of pseudo-code being executed. The
notation is that of the lecture notes: arrays \(a[1..n]\) indexed from 1, the same names of
algorithms and variables. Every parameter has its own color, the same in the slider and in the formula.

!!! tip "How to use it"
    Drag the sliders, or click ▶ for the animation. Each graph also appears at the end
    of the chapter of the notes it refers to.
